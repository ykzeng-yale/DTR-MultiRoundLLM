# E12 proposal: a discriminating development test on fresh, prospectively sampled roots (NOT RELEASED)

**Experiments workstream, as experimental lead.** E11 answered nothing about the hypothesis, because it reused
seven inspected roots and 6 of 7 were solved at STOP. E12 is the smallest study that can observe repair: it
runs the frozen five-arm design on **fresh roots sampled prospectively from the reviewed frame**.

## Frame and roots (fixed before any receiver call)

- **Roots:** the **18 `include` records** of the MRL-15 frame review (`results/frame_review_mrl15_*`, records
  sha `96dad503…`), in their seeded order:

  918, 825, 842, 31, 847, 816, 895, 868, 288, 154, 907, 863, 966, 652, 963, 651, 499, 974.

  The two holds (359 and 349) are excluded before collection for a stated specification reason. There is no
  replacement and no outcome-based screen; this is lead ruling 3's default design.
- **Endpoint.** For each root:
  - the public example shown to every arm is MBPP `test_list[0]`, the historical visible-example convention;
  - the private suite is `test_list[1:]`;
  - the negative control is the pool screen's do-nothing stub, already shown to fail each root's own tests.

  The public and private cases do not overlap by construction. This thinner suite is a known limitation to
  record: there are no authored boundary cases.
- **Instrument:** grader v5 and the public checker as validated at 174/200 (E6′ and E8′ pass). The frozen
  arms are STOP, N0, S0, N1, S1 and R1 with R = 2; seeds are independent `arm:replicate` streams.

**Needs a new budget:** 174 + 252 = 426 starts, above the 200 ledger. This is the only real resource
decision.

## What would be informative

A test is informative only if some roots fail at STOP. At the 3B's historical first-attempt rate of about
0.6, roughly 7 of 18 would. Among initially failing roots, S1−N1 and N1−N0 measure whether seeing, and being
directed by, the executed public diagnostic changes repair. On initially passing roots, the same contrasts
measure damage.

Everything is reported descriptively, with root-level vectors and no population intervals. E12 is still
development evidence: roughly 18 roots in about 18 provisional families.
