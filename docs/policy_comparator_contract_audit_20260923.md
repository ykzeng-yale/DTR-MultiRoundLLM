# Policy comparator contract: the unresolved fair comparison

Coordinating scientific lead, 23 September 2026. **Read-only design audit**, using the [proposed independent-policy trial](independent_prompt_policy_validation_design_20260922.md), [target fork](policy_trial_decision_fork_20260923.md), and saved E12/E13a/E14 contracts. This is not a policy freeze, new empirical analysis or collection release. The original target is a history-conditional *next-prompt* choice under a fixed receiver, followed by independent policy validation. The existing negative development results remain intact.

## One decision time, three policies

At a proposed landmark, all policies must be handed the same frozen task, initial receiver artifact and permitted public diagnostic. Let that complete public record be `X`. Before any evaluation outcomes, define the action set as exact continuation recipes, including their full message arrays, context retention, common terminal instruction and seed/decoding law. A prompt-choice comparison requires the selector and fixed comparator to choose **one** recipe from that same supported set at that same checkpoint. The selector cannot view a private grade or another branch's output. The fixed recipe must be selected on development data and frozen; calling N1 “competent” solely because it is familiar or because E12 tied S1 is not a selection rule.

| Policy | Decision-time information and action | What must be charged to hypothetical deployment | Unresolved here |
|---|---|---|---|
| `d` (public-history selector) | `X` only; one frozen deterministic mapping into the supported continuation recipes; no evaluation-label tuning | Initial generation, public check, feature computation, selection, chosen continuation and any authorized acceptance check | Feature bytes, mapping, support and training split |
| `b1` (competent fixed continuation) | Receives the same `X` and one development-selected recipe; it may ignore `X` | Same common initial/check work plus its continuation and checks | Development selection procedure and exact recipe |
| `b2` (independent-sampling controller) | Same initial public record for the landmark quality trial, then its own frozen, deployment-visible redraw/check/stopping history; never private grades or another branch's outputs | All operations required by its declared policy and eligibility law, including initial generation once; an unused diagnostic is trial expenditure but need not be its end-to-end deployment cost | Whether it restarts from bare task or redraws from the full checkpoint, its public-check/eligibility law, maximum calls, acceptance rule and total budget |

The [E13a FRESH arm](e13_gated_policy_proposal_20260922.md) is bare-task resampling, whereas its R1 arm also carries a diagnostic and instruction. Its five development-selected checkpoints and six draws per arm do **not** freeze `b2` or establish its policy value. The proposed [E14](e14_proposal_v4_20260923.md) compares N1 with a public-status-dependent S1 at identical full prefixes; both share a terminal instruction. It has no independent-sampling arm, and its ten histories would be descriptive development data. E14's task *sources* were reviewed, but its proposed receiver prefixes are fresh; v4's phrase “already-exposed histories” is stale against the later worker correction in [COORDINATION](../COORDINATION.md). Reusing E14 labels to claim the three-policy trial would change the estimand.

## Decision that must precede a numerical freeze

Specify `b2` as a *complete* algorithm, including exactly where each redraw starts, what public bytes it sees, how it stops or accepts, and its maximum resources. A bare-task restart and a redraw retaining the full history answer different questions. Match the **available total deployment resource** and public information, then report realized calls, tokens, latency, energy and money separately. The [prospective cost correction](policy_cost_counterfactual_contract_20260924.md) separates trial expenditure, incremental cost after `X`, and end-to-end cost from the task. Constructing a diagnostic for every trial branch does not imply that a controller which ignores it would pay for it in unconditional deployment; if the evaluation/eligibility law requires that diagnostic, charge it to that controller too. Freeze the eligibility law and cost starting line before outcomes. Equal continuation-call counts alone do not make a practical comparison fair; forcing `b2` to spend unused budget is also unnecessary. The `d`–`b1` quality contrast can remain the prompt-choice mechanism test even if a fair `b2` cannot yet be frozen, but practical superiority must then remain open.

The next protocol must also certify untouched independent families, equal-family weights or another declared target, endpoint validity, policy-specific branch execution, seed/assignment laws, missing outcomes, simultaneous inference, the `.05` useful-gain rule and a complete local cap. The existing 198-root mechanically screened frame does not provide that certificate. If a smaller finite frame is chosen, label it as the changed finite-frame target before collecting outcomes. A source/mock or real-instrument feasibility check earns no policy-effect claim.

**Decision: HOLD new E14 and policy collection.** This audit identifies a missing comparator definition rather than supplying it. No model calls, tokens, benchmark/reference/candidate execution, fit, synthetic draw or paid spend occurred. The original same-prefix question, E12 −1/28 development result and E13a 9/30 versus 12/30 restart-package result are unchanged. **58%, change 0 percentage points** on the fixed rubric; prompt efficacy is unestablished and the full project is not submission-ready.
