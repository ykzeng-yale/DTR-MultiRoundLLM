# LEAD-POLICY-08: separate policy decision from a 0.05-width preference

Coordinating scientific lead, 25 September 2026. **Same-target repair of our planning interpretation, with no trial or model release.** [LEAD-POLICY-07](policy_source_ceiling_20260925.md) correctly shows that MBPP alone cannot attain a simultaneous `0.05` half-width under the already reviewed conservative two-contrast Hoeffding fallback. That fact must not be turned into a categorical impossibility claim for the original useful-benefit question. The [prospective policy design](independent_prompt_policy_validation_design_20260922.md) specifies a useful-gain threshold `g=0.05` and declares benefit when `L_1>g`, futility when `U_1<g`, and otherwise an inconclusive result. It does **not** logically require interval radius `<=g` for every possible sample outcome.

With complete independent, equally weighted family contrasts in `[-1,1]` from the specified sampling law, and only the two predeclared contrasts, the reviewed fallback has simultaneous radius `r_n=sqrt(2 log(80)/n)`. At hypothetical `n=544` distinct eligible families, `r_n=0.1269267192`; useful benefit is decidable if the observed family-average contrast exceeds `0.1769267192`, and useful-gain futility if it is below `-0.0769267192`. At the current 198-root convenience-frame *arithmetic ceiling*, the corresponding thresholds would be `0.2603874885` and `-0.1603874885`. Equalities satisfy neither strict rule. These are **prospective decision thresholds**, not observed effects, power calculations or intervals for E12/E14. The 544 and 198 lists do not certify the assumptions or supply untouched families. An intermediate observed effect, including `0.10` at `n=544`, would be inconclusive about `g` under this fallback.

**Decision: keep the 0.05 half-width as an explicit precision goal, not an automatic model-collection admission rule.** A smaller original-target independent-family trial could, in principle, decide a sufficiently large positive or negative effect. Before one is worth executing, the lead must justify its chance of a useful decision from *prospective* variance/effect scenarios and total cost, without treating E12's outcome-selected subgroup or seed replicates as independent-family power data. If the most plausible scenarios yield an almost certainly inconclusive result at the feasible family count, expand the source or justify another prespecified interval design rather than spend receiver calls. Do not switch to an optimistic asymptotic interval because it produces a desired sample size; justify coverage and family sampling before outcomes. A finite-frame target remains a changed hypothesis, not a silent route around sampling assumptions.

No selector, comparator, family frame, endpoint, versions/hashes, assignment law, failure rule, cost controller, useful-gain/futility and inference freeze or numerical local cap is complete. E14 N1/S1 remains NO-GO as an efficacy stage; MBPP-only source screening remains on hold as a way to **meet 0.05 width**, but this note corrects any reading that all smaller original-target trials are impossible. No worker job or model allowance is issued. The E11/E12/E13a negatives are preserved. This is a reused-design numerical check, not new efficacy evidence, a new theorem or a simulation. New model calls, tokens, benchmark runs, fits and paid charges: zero. Prompt efficacy unestablished; full project not submission-ready.
