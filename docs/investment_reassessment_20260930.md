# Fresh scientific and investment reassessment

Codex coordinating lead, 30 September 2026. The owner requested a second thorough audit of design, outcomes, theory, novelty and further investment. Three parallel internal reviewers independently challenged the empirical, design and theoretical conclusions. This is a reassessment of saved evidence, not a new policy trial, external peer review or retrospective endpoint repair. It extends rather than replaces the earlier [investment review](investment_review_20260930.md).

## Decision

**Do not scale compute, architecture or open-ended infrastructure. One bounded empirical reset remains defensible, conditional on a qualified instrument, usable development/evaluation separation and informative prospective decision.** The central hypothesis—an actual learned public-history next-prompt/STOP policy improves on competent complete strategies—has not been tested. It has neither positive validation nor a general futility result. Sunk resources and a 60% artifact-readiness score provide no investment rationale.

For a project whose main ambition is new statistical theory, the current novelty case is weak. For a carefully scoped applied-methods study of automated user-side interventions, there is a plausible contribution, but its decisive empirical evidence is absent. No publication probability or guaranteed rescue is implied.

## Design and evaluation: what went wrong

The coordinating lead owns the sequence. It was not adequately designed throughout. Early routing, fixed repair and bank-selection results answered adjacent questions while the project aimed at personalized next-prompt intervention. Subsequent small exposed panels improved execution and diagnosis without delivering the actual fit-and-holdout experiment. Excessive infrastructure work and the later blanket claim that no scoped empiricism was possible delayed the decisive test. Those failures cannot be assigned to Claude or lack of GPUs.

Complete execution of fixed intervention packages can legitimately estimate their finite contrasts without action-propensity weights. It does not identify arbitrary generator changes or demonstrate a deployable conditional policy. The only correct sequential STOP/PATCH/RETHINK logger contains 18 trajectories and 31 decisions on nine reused roots, with second-stage action cells of 2/4/7 histories. It cannot support credible training plus independent evaluation of six 256-feature regressions. [Existing-data fitability audit](existing_full_history_fitability_audit_20260929.md).

The recorded evaluation is substantially more trustworthy than the original semantic interpretation:

* Historical empty-check stopping accepted 714 episodes, 424 of which failed the full benchmark tests. This is false reassurance from an uninformative public tool, not an LLM's demonstrated introspective ability. It does not alone explain the repair–resampling contrast because the initial gate is shared.
* E12 task863 passed private examples despite a duplicate-handling defect; directed failures on that root were frozen extraction/format failures. They are not evidence that a successful semantic repair damaged a previously correct program.
* Replication case records include 500 process errors and 41 invalid observations; strategy records include 64 process errors and six invalid observations. Under the frozen taxonomy their unavailable outcomes remain unavailable. They are not automatically infrastructure outages, wrong answers or semantic treatment damage.
* Original-example lookup controls intentionally contain original answers. Original-private diagnostics are not unseen validation. Correct-reference and wrong-control discrimination validates a finite instrument against those controls, not every possible program.

No historical regrading or failure deletion is warranted. In a new study, operational program failure and unavailable observation must be distinguished prospectively. If the endpoint is a finite executable test grade, a completed syntax/runtime/resource failure can be an observed deployment failure under a newly frozen rule; observer or infrastructure unavailability remains missing. Fallback/final-artifact rules require the same freeze. The current development interface's abort-on-any-INCOMPLETE rule is honest but fragile until this distinction is operational. It cannot justify relabeling old INCOMPLETE outcomes.

Comparator coverage improved, but equal call ceilings did not equal realized resource use. Reflexion adaptation used 9,866 continuation prompt tokens and 4,151 completion tokens; resampling used 3,612 and 1,859. Charge logical initial costs to each policy despite physical reuse. Critique consumes a call and leaves fewer executable attempts; disclose this as a complete budget-limited strategy, not a reproduction of the original method. Tokens, latency, dollars and unmeasured energy remain distinct. No total-cost superiority is established.

## Theory and innovation

The reviewed current identities are coherent under their stated assumptions; the internal review found no new substantive algebra defect in those reviewed identities. This is not certification of every proof or of empirical assumptions. G-formula, IPW, longitudinal DR, Q-learning, performance-difference bounds, policy gradients and bounded-mean concentration are established machinery.

Close primary precedents narrow the novelty claim: [CausalCollab](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.91/) studies longitudinal human–LM refinement; [Zhang, Wang and Dhillon (2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.17538v1) explicitly formulates natural-language DTR/Q-learning; [Kiyohara et al. (2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.02646v1) learns personalized prompts for a frozen LLM from logged feedback; [C3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.06859v2) uses restored histories and alternative continuation rollouts. This is a targeted closest-work assessment, not an exhaustive priority search or replication of their findings.

The possible contribution is a validated supported user-side intervention system with full-history choices, absorbing STOP, controlled information access, honest policy training, reliable public/private outcomes, direct deployment and whole-strategy cost comparison. Moving the controlled speaker to the user side is meaningful, but not new DTR mathematics. A support/version audit or working software alone does not establish a major methodological contribution.

Three gaps are particularly important:

1. A causal interpretation comes from the design and assumptions, not a predictor's name. Correct full-history Q regression is a serious comparator. Lossy hashed text features do not become sufficient merely because all messages are passed into the encoder.
2. Current code is quality-only standard backward fitted Q with IPW/DR **evaluation**, not DR-trained optimization. DR can reduce bias while increasing variance. Synthetic weak-overlap RMSE .09872 versus regression .07296 provides no DR-superiority mandate.
3. Policy-improvement bounds assume uniform critic accuracy over reachable histories; candidate coverage bounds assume adequate probability of near-optimal supported proposals. Neither has been established for real language. Formal positive probability is not practical overlap. These theorems explain what would suffice, not why the controller will succeed.

The new conditional finite-census concentration argument is legitimate under fresh independent complete-execution vectors and a fixed pre-evaluation law. Repetition improves execution precision for that roster; it does not create new semantic families or broad transport. Unknown receiver pretraining and incomplete broad semantic taxonomy limit claims, but do not prohibit a narrowly declared fixed-receiver randomized study. Do not recreate impossible universal-correctness or unseen-pretraining admission requirements.

The manuscript still juxtaposes an older two-flag landmark learner and the prospective full-history/STOP target. Before collection it needs one coherent primary target, with historical landmark studies clearly separated. A quality-only controller also cannot claim cost-optimal STOP without a specified cost objective or an independently demonstrated quality/resource tradeoff.

## One decisive remaining study

The preserved broad research question is whether allowed public conversation history predicts useful next-prompt choices, including STOP, beyond competent alternatives. A separately claimed finite-source illustration can test this without pretending to establish broad-family or human-user transport.

Complete the instrument/source gate under a hard prospective envelope; use outcome-blind operational co-splits and a historical controller-exposure crosswalk. Freeze separate development, tuning and untouched evaluation groups, the randomized supported behavior slate, meaningful action-cell/complexity diagnostics, response/program representation, public/private information, operational failures/fallbacks, receiver and execution law. No exposed nine-root fit or task replacement selected from policy outcomes.

Fit and lock an actual modest public-history policy. Include a matched public-status/compressed-history learner to test whether history itself contributes; an ordinary fitted-Q method remains legitimate. If corrected-label/DR training is added, it is a separately specified method needing its own comparison, not a prerequisite imposed by terminology.

Directly deploy the locked policies and development-locked competent B1, public-selected resampling B2 and STOP from matched fresh natural starts. Information access, final-artifact rules and resource allowances must be declared for every complete strategy. Preserve the primary `L1 > .05` useful-gain rule, `U1 < .05` scoped futility and B2 superiority `L2 > 0`; cost benefit requires a separate pinned analysis. Resolve informative precision and freeze the finite assignment/call/time/storage cap before evaluation, rather than infer power from an all-zero radius or reused family count. OPE can corroborate direct deployment, not replace it.

**Decision at the cap:** source/instrument/fit infeasibility closes this empirical line; a qualified upper bound below useful gain closes the declared regime; a wide result is inconclusive and does not automatically authorize expansion. A useful independently evaluated gain could justify a separately planned replication. It would still not validate unrestricted prompt optimization or broad transport. No positive result is required to preserve a scientifically honest conclusion.
