# LEAD-POLICY-07: MBPP-only source ceiling for the existing policy precision plan

Coordinating scientific lead, 25 September 2026. **Decision: do not commission further MBPP-only semantic screening as a path to the original independent-family trial under the currently specified conservative 0.05 half-width.** This is a read-only source-and-arithmetic feasibility decision, not a policy result, a new theorem, or a decision to abandon the original question.

The committed [full-source acquisition audit](landmark_task_source_candidates_20260920.md) counts 974 official MBPP IDs; 427 are in the prior canonical pool. Of the 547 other IDs, three have normalized descriptions identical to prior roots, leaving **544 candidate IDs**. This is an *optimistic ceiling*, not a certified evaluation frame. Some of these candidate roots have since entered E11/E12 development or E14 preparation, so the untouched count is lower: semantic family overlap, source exposure, task validity, grader discrimination and public/private separation can only reduce its usable independent-family count. The currently reviewed 198-root convenience frame is a subset with additional unresolved adjudication.

The existing, independently reviewed [two-contrast bounded-family inference fallback](independent_prompt_policy_validation_design_20260922.md) uses simultaneous radius `sqrt(2 log(80)/n)` for `n` independent, equally weighted, complete family contrasts in `[-1,1]`, conditional on a frozen target-aligned family sampling design. **Even granting all 544 candidate IDs distinct eligible independent families**, that radius is `0.1269267192`; the stated `0.05` radius requires `3,506` families. The source count and substitution were checked against committed JSON and evaluated separately with ordinary and 50-digit decimal arithmetic. The radius is a planning bound, **not** a confidence interval for current data or a guarantee of power. The assumptions of independence, eligibility and population sampling are *not established* for the 544 IDs. This calculation neither precludes observing a large effect nor rules out a prospectively justified, more efficient inference procedure or a deliberately narrower finite-frame estimand.

For the **original family-population claim with the present 0.05 conservative planning target**, screening additional MBPP candidate IDs cannot close the sample-size gap. The next lead-owned choice is therefore to establish an additional defensible task-family source and sampling law, or to justify and freeze a different uncertainty/precision contract *before outcomes*. A finite-frame evaluation of a smaller audited roster is a changed, explicitly labeled target; it cannot silently inherit the original population-policy claim. Each route still needs the supported public-history action set, frozen selector and fair fixed/independent-sampling comparators, endpoint, assignment and failure laws, versions/hashes, useful-gain and futility rules, resource lease and complete numerical local cap. No E14 source renewal, model collection or duplicate worker job is released.

Evidence classification: reused committed source census and prospective numerical planning. Actual new model calls/tokens, benchmark executions, synthetic draws, fits and paid charges: zero. E11/E12/E13a negatives remain as recorded. Prompt efficacy is unestablished; independent policy validation is absent; the full project is not submission-ready.
