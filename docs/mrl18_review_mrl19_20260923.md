## Lead-owned analysis and sizing repairs

The original analyzer kept missing counts but computed its main contrast from available grades. With one R1 pass and five missing grades versus three FRESH passes and three failures, it reports +0.5 although the assigned-slot contrast may lie anywhere from −1/3 to +0.5. Retaining missing counts alone does not protect the estimand.

For a cell with `a` assigned outcomes, `s` passes and `m` missing, the finite mean lies in `[s/a, (s+m)/a]`. A root contrast is bounded by `[L_R1−U_FRESH, U_R1−L_FRESH]`; averaging both endpoints equally across all five roots bounds the finite assigned-checkpoint contrast. These bounds are exact over binary completions, require no missing-at-random assumption, and are **not confidence intervals** for receiver expectations or a task population. The revised primary point contrast is unavailable if any assigned primary grade is missing. Available-case summaries are explicitly secondary; missing grades are not observed failures. Complete-data results are unchanged.

The sizing revisions correct three distinct problems:

- A two-observation Bernoulli sample variance can equal 0.5. The observed average within variance of 0.30 does not violate a population variance ceiling of 0.25. Neither the noisy 0.025 residual nor a 0.05 sensitivity assumption is an identified lower bound on between-root variance.
- Under the stated hypothetical normal approximation, declaring a useful gain at a true effect equal to the threshold has probability 0.025. Effects at or below 0.05 cannot supply 80% useful-benefit power under that approximation; they do not make a favorable declaration mathematically impossible. The 50% power inversion must use the interval critical value 1.96, not zero. An infeasible 80% futility inversion is reported as unavailable, not as zero gain meeting the target.
- The STOP-only mixture identity is valid when the policy contrast is exactly zero outside the gate. A fixed-continuation comparator changes both gated and non-gated contrasts, so it can increase or decrease variance. The existing STOP calculations do not size the required fixed-continuation comparison.

These corrections preserve **hold-new-stage for E13b as proposed**, without claiming that every larger-effect study is impossible. Five public-diagnostic-selected development checkpoints and unresolved sampling/family structure do not establish a gate-prevalence confidence interval or transport to the remaining source frame; the follow-up intervention was chosen after inspecting outcomes. The larger generator and independent-policy gates remain closed.
