# Investment review and correction of the empirical-design blockade

Codex scientific lead, 30 September 2026. Owner-requested assessment of positive signals, design/evaluation quality, and further investment. This is a review and recommendation, not an experiment, population adoption, or collection release.

## Recommendation

Do not expand the current compute, generator-training, or infrastructure program. One bounded redesign/feasibility effort is defensible because the central learned-prompt-policy hypothesis has not actually received its intended test. Further model compute requires a concrete, informative, fully frozen study. If that cannot be assembled, close empirical expansion and preserve an honest methods/development/negative-results account; publication is not assured.

Past investment produced useful evidence and reusable software, but no demonstrated learned public-history prompt/STOP advantage over competent fixed prompting and independent resampling. Sunk compute is not a reason to continue. Negative outcomes from tested recipes remain negative.

## Design and evaluation were not adequate throughout

The lead owns the research sequence. Early data answered receiver-routing/fixed-repair questions while the project aimed at next-prompt personalization. Architecture and infrastructure preceded adequate randomized development data, measurement, and independent evaluation. Repeated small panels improved debugging and finite descriptions without creating new independent families. The recent status-only loop was also a leadership failure.

Saved assignment/grade arithmetic and provenance have been substantially reconciled; this supports recorded finite endpoints. Semantic correctness was not established. Historically, 90/561 tasks had no substantive visible checks; 424 of 714 load-only passes that stopped failed the full benchmark tests. E12 task 863 passed two private examples despite a duplicate-handling defect detected by a valid public example. Output-format failures were also components of some scores, not automatically demonstrated semantic damage. These limitations do not justify deleting failures or retrospectively repairing negative results. See [measurement judgment](measurement_judgment_20260920.md) and E12.

Precision also needs resolution before calls. In one frozen hypothetical law with true +10 pp, 30% disagreement and n=198, the conservative procedure cleared the +5 pp lower-bound criterion in only .00150 of simulations. This is conditional synthetic behavior, not actual power or a universal sample-size requirement. Preserve that run/rule; any new prospective inference specification requires justification and freeze while retaining the five-point target. See [operating-characteristics result](paired_interval_operating_characteristics_results_20260929.md).

## Correction: limited generalization does not prohibit empirical work

The [sampling-law ruling](broad_task_family_sampling_law_decision_20260930.md) was too restrictive in making owner-provided prospective task traffic the sole route to empirical reopening. An unspecified broad family law prevents inference about that law. It does not prohibit an internally valid randomized study with an explicitly defined finite-source/domain estimand. The original [protocol](experiment_protocol.md) contemplated a benchmark feasibility batch and limited its generalization; the measurement judgment explicitly recognized a narrow benchmark estimand.

A scoped study would be a separately versioned empirical target illustrating the broader framework. It would not validate all task families or silently replace the original objective. No source is adopted, old exclusion reversed, or old outcome reclassified here. Source choice must be justified prospectively by relevance, intervention opportunity and measurement feasibility, not favorable policy results.

Zero *currently admitted* roots is a ledger status, not proof that no task can ever be usable. Absence of publisher challenge tests does not prove that prospectively authored, separately validated evaluation tests are impossible. Unknown receiver pretraining exposure differs from controller development/evaluation leakage: it limits novel-task/transport claims and may create ceiling effects, but does not automatically invalidate randomized contrasts for a fixed receiver. Stronger tests still define a bounded instrument, not all-input semantic proof. The primary [EvalPlus study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.01210) demonstrates that test augmentation can uncover errors and change rankings; it does not validate our tasks or authorize adopting another dataset.

The gate is a concrete scoped design with adequate measurement and separation, not universal representativeness. Prospective task traffic is one possible route. This correction supersedes the exclusive-stream requirement, while preserving the lack of broad-target identification.

## Conditions for another investment

1. Explicit source/domain/estimand, outcome-blind family split and exposure accounting, with separately validated public/private measurement qualified for those task types.
2. Supported randomized development trajectories and an actual modest public-history/STOP fit. Regression and any DR-trained method must be compared fairly; DR superiority is not a prerequisite imposed by branding.
3. Locked evaluation on held-out controller-evaluation families versus STOP, competent fixed/heuristic prompting and public-selected resampling. Charge all initial, generator, critic, check and selection costs. Label adaptations accurately.
4. Informative prospective precision/decision analysis, fixed call/time/resource cap, and no favorable-outcome task replacement or endpoint/threshold changes. A strict upper bound below +.05 supports useful-gain futility only for the declared contrast/regime; wide intervals remain inconclusive. An inconclusive capped result does not automatically authorize expansion.

This is a recommendation, not a frozen plan or guaranteed rescue. If these cannot be made concrete within one pre-agreed feasibility effort, open-ended compute is a poor investment. A favorable scoped result could justify a separately planned replication/transport study, not a broad claim.
