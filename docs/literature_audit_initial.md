# Literature audit and defensible positioning

Audit date: **2026-09-19**. Scope: the named papers and linked sources in the supplied conversation, plus targeted searches for adjacent work. This is an article-level verification audit, not an exhaustive systematic review. Primary sources are author manuscripts, publisher/proceedings records, and official author repositories. A published method's reported guarantee or empirical advantage is not independently reproduced by this audit.

## Main conclusion

The proposed system combines several established ideas: adaptive regimes, sequential causal value estimation, prompt generation, and iterative interaction. None is novel by itself. The most defensible proposed contribution is an **experimentally identifiable, support-aware framework for history-specific prompt decisions and continuation-policy value in multi-round interaction with a fixed receiver**, including randomized selection among newly generated free-form prompts, explicit generator/selector scope, adaptive stopping, and independent evaluation after learning.

This is a candidate contribution, not an established priority claim. The search did not establish that no earlier paper combines these elements. The implementation and theory must demonstrate what the proposed design changes beyond applying standard longitudinal estimators to a new domain.

## Corrections to the supplied idea

1. **Causal inference and RL are overlapping literatures.** It is incorrect to mark all multi-turn RL or off-policy evaluation as noncausal. Jiang and Li explicitly interpret off-policy evaluation as counterfactual policy evaluation; DTR Q-learning also connects directly to RL. The distinction here is the intervention, identification assumptions, estimand, and evaluation design, not a blanket causal-versus-RL label.
2. **“Individualized” is not a literal unit-level effect.** A value conditioned on a conversation history averages future receiver randomness and, where applicable, unobserved heterogeneity among compatible histories. Repeated independent continuations estimate conditional means; they do not reveal the joint counterfactual pair for one realized stochastic trajectory.
3. **Embeddings do not automatically define valid interventions.** Semantic similarity does not imply equal causal responses. A map from text to a vector requires a version rule and a sufficiency assumption to identify effects of that vector. A treatment encoder is a modeling device unless these assumptions are justified.
4. **Neural/orthogonal does not mean identification without assumptions.** DeepBlip and DRQ have specific statistical models and nuisance requirements. Their guarantees do not transfer automatically to a compressed conversation history, arbitrary new prompts, hidden user intent, or a changing receiver.
5. **Time-varying confounding does not make ordinary sequential outcome regression intrinsically invalid.** Correctly specified g-computation is a valid comparator. A deliberately history-ablated reward model cannot be the only non-DR baseline.
6. **Prompt generation and virtual-user interaction already exist.** Self-Refine, ProRefine, PHP, prompt optimizers, and simulator work occupy this engineering space. A new architecture diagram alone does not establish a methods contribution.
7. **Text-as-treatment research is older than 2026.** TextCause (2021) already formalizes causal effects of linguistic properties and the associated proxy/measurement problem.
8. **A generator update changes the target intervention distribution.** Selection probabilities within old menus do not establish support for an arbitrary new generator. Randomized menu selection identifies the fixed-generator selector target; changed generators need their joint likelihood ratio under suitable support or fresh experiments.
9. The attachment's `arcxiv.org/abs/2608.17499` is a spelling error. FACA was verified at **arxiv.org**. The supplied OpenReview DRQ link encountered a browser-verification challenge; the matching author manuscript and its publication statement were accessible. An access failure is not a paywall finding.

## Proposed contribution and falsifiable evidence

**Claim that is supportable now:** a concrete research program joining longitudinal causal evaluation with user-side multi-round prompting, with explicit assumptions and a reproducible experimental contract.

**Proposed methodological contribution:** define text interventions through a logged proposal distribution and a history-adaptive selector; identify and estimate continuation-specific value under that exact intervention distribution; show the boundary where novel-generator or compressed-semantic claims become unsupported; connect conditional value learning to independently assessed cost-sensitive prompting and STOP.

**Evidence required before a stronger claim:**

- Known-truth experiments demonstrate the estimator's stated robustness and its failures under hidden confounding, support loss, or history aliasing.
- A strong correctly conditioned sequential-regression comparator receives the same representation and tuning budget as the orthogonal critic.
- Randomized free-form slates show conditional-value/ranking calibration at held-out histories; generated text must vary beyond a fixed small category list.
- A frozen policy improves prospectively evaluated task outcomes or the quality–cost frontier at equal total compute and information access.
- A changed generator is evaluated using newly collected trajectories or a justified full proposal/selection likelihood ratio. Old selection propensities alone are insufficient.
- Generator-only and selector-only ablations distinguish better candidate coverage from better causal ranking. Report critic-predicted gains versus fresh-execution gains to expose overoptimization.

If a DR critic does not improve a well-specified sequential-regression comparator, that is informative: randomized identification and honest policy evaluation may still be useful, while superiority of the learning algorithm would remain unsupported. If performance gains vanish at equal compute or with hidden evaluators removed, report that result rather than redefining the target.

## Search scope and unresolved verification

Targeted query families included exact titles and identifiers, `dynamic treatment regime prompt LLM`, `longitudinal causal prompting language models`, `causal multi-turn prompt optimization`, and `sequential causal prompt optimization`, plus exact-acronym resolution. The audit checked each specific named method and every unique linked paper/repository in the supplied text, including resolving unlinked SMTPO, Reflexion, CRN, and LATS. It also added TextCause, core DR/OPE references, and the 2026 edit-level paper.

Remaining limitations: this is not a citation-network census; no baseline code was run; most baseline scopes were checked against abstracts and method descriptions, with deeper methods checks for CPO, DRQ, DeepBlip, and latent textual treatments. DRQ's supplied OpenReview identifier remains inaccessible through that service in this audit, although matching title/authors/arXiv publication statement were verified. Venue status marked “arXiv reports” should be replaced with the final proceedings record during manuscript submission. No “first,” “only,” or “no prior work” claim is justified by this search.
