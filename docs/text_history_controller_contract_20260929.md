# Practical public-text controller implementation, before fitting

The unresolved scientific link is a learned full-history/STOP rule trained on actual supported trajectories. Prior full-history pilot merely randomized actions; pooled synthetic integer-history regression was not a text policy. `experiments/sequential/text_history_q.py` now implements a fixed 256-dimensional signed-hash map over the complete ordered public transcript, public statuses and remaining budget, then fits action-specific ridge Q regressions backward for two decisions. STOP's Q uses observed STOP labels; continuing first-turn targets use fitted second-turn max Q. Ties choose STOP. It accepts only the exact STOP/PATCH/RETHINK realized slate with bound full logging probabilities and exact prompt chronology. Receiver, generator and public-observer versions must match. Missing terminal outcomes, early service failure misclassified as STOP, absent stage-action samples and train/evaluation family overlap cause refusal. It is standard Q-learning, not a new causal theorem. The hash representation is lossy and not proven sufficient for language histories.

The module also computes known-propensity IPW and longitudinal augmented (DR) scores for a **fixed fitted Q policy** on disjoint families. DR here evaluates a policy; it does not train or optimize a DR policy. The usual causal interpretation assumes consistency, sequential randomization by construction at every realized supported slate, no hidden outcome-dependent censoring, and useful finite-sample overlap; family independence and source/measurement validity still need justification. Estimated Q alone cannot rescue absent actions. The score rows do not supply uncertainty intervals or cost benefit. Tokens, latency, money and energy need separate collection and analysis. No outcome was fitted in this wake, including the nine exposed pilot roots; tests use constructed deterministic contract data only.

The saved pilot has 18 first decisions (STOP5/PATCH6/RETHINK7) and 13 second decisions (STOP2/PATCH4/RETHINK7) across nine reused roots. These small cells and unique text histories provide no credible independent policy ranking or cost claim. Before actual fit, complete family/exposure admission and private-label join, freeze all development and evaluation families separately, add missingness accounting, source-isolated native observation, feature/penalty specification and cost-aware comparison. A completed learner artifact and independent frozen evaluation remain required.
