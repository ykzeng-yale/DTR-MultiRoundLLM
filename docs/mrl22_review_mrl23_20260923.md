## Target and inference decisions

For fixed realized histories H=(h1,...,hG), distinguish the conditional response-mean parameter

`theta_H = (1/G) sum_g [ E(Y_D | h_g, frozen receiver law) - E(Y_N | h_g, frozen receiver law) ]`

from its all-assigned R-draw sample contrast. A sample statistic does not become a causal mean merely by definition. Consistency/state restoration, no interference and the declared receiver-sampling assumptions are needed for the mean interpretation. Label deterministic seeds and their derivation as reproducibility settings, not a proof of independent random draws. Every branch slot is included with probability1 under the complete-branch design; this is not a binary treatment propensity and requires no inverse-propensity weighting.

Report the finite sample contrast, per-root counts and all-assigned missing-completion bounds **descriptively**. No normal confidence interval, Hoeffding interval, efficacy or futility declaration is released for this small development stage. Hoeffding requires independence (or an appropriate dependence-specific result); the proposed radius is not assumption-free. The hypothetical normal scenarios neither prove adequate precision nor prove that precision is impossible. Their sensitivity arithmetic is planning material only.

A fixed task roster can also have a well-defined mean over fresh-prefix randomness. It does not require random task sampling merely to define that target. Broader task-population generalization needs a population/sampling or transport argument; independent policy value needs a separate frozen policy design. E12 already used source-fixed tasks, fresh prefixes and information-matched N1/S1: the prospective changes here are different development tasks, explicitly adapted measurement, and a shared terminal instruction, not a new design class.

Caps fixed without inspecting scores prohibit score-driven stopping; they do not make missingness score-independent. Runtime and failures may correlate with potential scores. Keep every assignment, suppress the primary point contrast if any primary branch grade is missing, and report binary completion bounds without a missing-at-random assumption. A failed initial generation leaves its twelve continuation slots missing; do not fabricate an answer, substitute a task-only intervention, retry or remove the root. A valid but incomplete public diagnostic uses a predeclared public-only incomplete-information instruction; environment/containment or receiver-law failures retain their stop rules.
