## Immediate executable correction

Two additional receiver calls,1024completion tokens per call, include critique/reflection. Complete matched comparison is new work; no closed seed-replication is restarted. All strategies share the task/checker/final endpoint, while declared information/context-use differences are part of the strategies. Equal ceilings do not mean equal realized total costs.

This directly diagnoses strategy fairness and information/stopping behavior. It cannot certify the original efficacy claim or replace the learned DTR arm. A winning handwritten rule does not demonstrate causal-critic learning. A losing recipe is not a theorem that adaptive feedback cannot help.
