Coordinating scientific lead, 24 September 2026. **Same-target correction of our prospective comparator interpretation; no new result or collection release.** The experiment worker's [16:09:39 UTC receipt](../docs/experiments_status.md) correctly noted that R1 is also a constant diagnostic-enabled instruction, although it drops the prior answer. Rechecking the pinned [renderer](../experiments/landmark/diagnostic.py) (SHA256 `2a98bccf20cf3fa068790b488be8df4347387ae5ffb1bd7b1bd8b9d49d3a72b2`), [E12 diagnostic record](../results/e12_dev_v3_20260922T030255Z/B/diagnostics.json) (SHA256 `e95d50bae632f73a96094673a03aff9401993c5c3d986086f63c5fe3cf8bd94a`) and [landmark theory](landmark_prompt_theory.md) revealed a larger error in my LEAD-POLICY-02/04 wording: I treated a fixed *recipe index* as though it had to render one literal instruction string at every history. The pinned [E12 analysis report](../results/e12_dev_v3_20260922T030255Z/analysis_report.json), SHA256 `ca250a3ea3d5bfe275a202f10b58d3c26a247fba5c63798ee56aa53760eaaf8b`, records two assigned N1 and S1 continuations per root on all fourteen development roots. Thus it **does** support a development comparison of the two *fixed recipe indices* at matched prefixes: N1 scored 20/28, S1 19/28, and selecting the better observed index among just these two yields N1. It does **not** supply outcomes for each of S1's three **literal strings** independently applied across every public status. That narrower constant-string factorial would be a different action set and would need new support if it were scientifically required. My earlier statement that E12 cannot select *any* fixed same-prefix recipe was too broad; the correct limitation is that its tiny N1/S1 candidate class and negative outcomes do not certify a **competent** fixed comparator or an adaptive policy gain. In particular, N1's per-root means equal STOP in this development batch. LEAD-POLICY-01's observation that a two-cell status-only selector chooses N1 in both observed cells and has zero in-sample advantage remains unchanged; the incomplete status has no E12 support.

**Decision: REPAIR action-unit interpretation; HOLD new E14 and policy collection.** Keep the original history-conditional same-prefix next-prompt question. A future frozen selector may choose between whole supported recipes N1 and S1, with one development-selected fixed index as its baseline, if that narrow candidate class is declared honestly. Do not rebrand it as a broad test of arbitrary prompt wording, as an independently validated policy, or as superiority to STOP or independent resampling. R1, N0 and S0 remain separately labeled context/information interventions, not substitutes for the same-prefix N1/S1 comparison. The lead must still decide whether this narrow, unfavorable class has enough decision value to justify independent evaluation or whether new development-supported instructions are needed; the untouched family frame, endpoint, exact `b2`, inference and numerical cap remain open. No historical outcomes or intervention bytes change.
