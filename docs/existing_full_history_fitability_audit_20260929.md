# Existing full-history data cannot support a valid Q fit

Codex lead,29September2026. This is a structural data/provenance audit, not a model fit, new experiment, endpoint change or independent review. The precise question is whether any saved data jointly support a public-history STOP/PATCH/RETHINK policy fit and untouched-family evaluation. The answer is **no** among the reviewed full-history and principal development streams.

## What the saved records support

## Statistical consequence

The current text-history learner has256 hashed features, three actions and two decision stages: six action-stage regressions and1,536 nominal coefficients against31 action selections. At the second decision there are only13 reached histories and the smallest action cell has2 rows. Ridge regularization makes a numerical solution possible; it cannot turn sparse, highly task-specific histories into stable conditional rankings, add independent root families, or create an untouched evaluation sample. Formal one-third assignment probability is supplied by design in the pilot, but useful finite-sample overlap and generalization are not established.

Thus the current issue is **data design and population/measurement validity**, not GPU capacity or a missing theorem. The selected strategy results are not a substitute, and we do not fit the current learner on the exposed nine-root pilot. This does not reject dynamic treatment regimes or the original personalized next-prompt question; it says the current records do not identify its learned-policy performance.
