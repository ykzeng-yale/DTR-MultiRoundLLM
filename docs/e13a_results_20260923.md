**Two qualifications I found by interrogating this result, both of which weaken the headline.** Source:
`scripts/e13a_static_description.py` → `results/e13a_static_description_20260923.json` (6 tests, 0 executions).

1. **The sign depends entirely on one checkpoint.** Dropping mbpp/863 moves the mean from −0.100 to **+0.042**,
   a sign reversal. No other checkpoint flips it. The equally weighted mean over five fixed checkpoints is not
   robust to removing one of them, and should not be read as if it were.
2. **The arms differ sharply in output format, not only in answers.** Under the frozen extraction rule, **13 of
   30 R1 outputs fail to parse against 0 of 30 FRESH outputs**. On mbpp/966, R1 produced **no** extractable
   program in any of its six draws (0/6 parse, 0/6 pass). The frozen endpoint is the private-suite-**plus-format**
   score, so those are legitimate zeros — but the contrast substantially reflects format under that rule rather
   than solution quality, and it echoes the S1/R1 multi-block formatting failures already recorded for E12.
   I deliberately did **not** compute a pass rate among parseable outputs only: that conditions on a property
   realised after the continuation, which is post-treatment selection and not a causal contrast.

**Discipline checks, reported because a negative reading needs them.**

- **The instrument is not inert:** both outcomes occur, all ten cells produced more than one distinct output,
  and the largest single-checkpoint movement is 0.667.
- **Saturation limits what could move:** mbpp/863 FRESH sits at the ceiling (6/6), mbpp/652 is at the floor in
  **both** arms (0/6 and 0/6, so that checkpoint can express nothing), and mbpp/966 R1 is at the floor.
- **No post-treatment selection:** 60 assigned, 60 graded, 0 dropped after the continuation.

- **The direction reverses across checkpoints.** The diagnostic package helped clearly on 842 and hurt clearly
  on 863; 288 and 966 moved one draw against it; 652 is a tie at zero. An equally weighted mean of five fixed
  checkpoints is not a population effect, and five checkpoints with six draws cannot separate these.
- **This does not identify a mechanism.** R1 and FRESH differ in two ways at once — the diagnostic bytes and
  the instruction — so neither the negative mean nor the per-root reversals attribute anything to the
  diagnostic alone. That was true when a tie was the expected outcome and it stays true now that it is not.
- **It is not a futility finding** about the rule, the arm or the project, and it does not generalize beyond
  these five histories.

**Observation on mbpp/863, recorded without a causal claim.** FRESH passed 6/6 where R1 passed 2/6. The lead's
E12 judgment established that 863's initial program resets its run length at duplicate values, returning 2
where the public example requires 3, and that its private suite passes only because those private cases contain
no duplicates. R1 carries that public diagnostic; FRESH does not. Whether carrying it is what moved the private
score here is **not** established by this design, and this run cannot settle it.
