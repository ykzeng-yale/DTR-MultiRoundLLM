## Why the E14 proposal is not released

1. **Selection changes the question.** The nine proposed IDs—918,825,816,895,868,154,651,499,974—are exactly the public-pass complement of the five E13a public-fail checkpoints among E12's 14 retained roots. Saved diagnostics show all nine public cases pass; E12 also records their initial private-suite passes. Consequently every DIRECTED request selects `S1_STRINGS[2]`, the preservation instruction. This is a reused, outcome-inspected preservation/damage contrast, not a roster retained regardless of public status. Conditioning on pretreatment public status can define a legitimate narrower target, but does not supply the requested new prospective development roster. We decline the proposed 108-call batch rather than relabeling it as a repair study.
2. **The incremental intervention must be named correctly.** E11/E12 already compared N1/S1 at identical full prefixes and diagnostic bytes. The proposed added terminal-output directive changes the intervention instructions. With the same extractor and private suites, it does not change measurement. A common directive does not guarantee equal realized formatting, isolate a formatting mechanism or validate the endpoint. E13a R1 and FRESH both omitted the previous answer; removal was not a between-arm difference.
3. **The sizing calculation cannot justify this release.** At six draws and hypothetical between-root variance .025, the stated within-root term is `2*.25/6 = .08333`, larger than .025. Additional replicates do reduce that term; the claim that only additional roots narrow the approximation is false. Hypothetical independent-root normal moment scenarios are not validated confidence intervals for nine selected fixed histories. Zero-null power is not power to establish gain above the .05 useful-gain threshold, and a nonzero observed contrast can arise from sampling variation. Do not use these scenarios for efficacy, futility or stopping decisions.
4. **The operational contract is incomplete.** The 153-start sum is 27 validation +108 candidate +18 rechecks; adding the nine renewed containment starts yields at least 162 under those components. Any new-prefix design additionally needs initial generation, public checks and complete validation/accounting. Existing E13a code enforces five roots/60 calls and is not an E14 release. A deadline can interrupt a root block and leave an arm partial; all assigned slots need frozen missing-outcome handling. A prompt-token estimate from a different task mix is not an enforced bound or an observed cost.
5. **A reserved rank range is not independent validation.** Ranks 61–198 may remain a protected candidate reserve, but historical exposure, semantic family overlap, evaluation access and the policy split need separate audit. Provisional duplicate labels and hash ranks neither prove independent families nor uniform sampling.

The independent theory reviewer and implementation reviewer agree with these corrections. This critiques our development design and proposal, not only the code. The frozen original proposal remains available as a historical draft; this ruling controls advancement.
