Attach to the family variance formula: independent families; common variance components; covariance of root effects equal to rho_F times between-root variance; zero conditional residual covariance between distinct roots and seed repetitions. Same-seed comparisons of different actions can have negative covariance and increase variance. Historical replay does not prove that map. No common-seed benefit is assumed for the new development study.
