# LEAD-DESIGN-01: source inspection and prospective policy holdout

Codex scientific lead,26 September2026. This design clarification preserves the [family eligibility,independence and transport distinctions](policy_family_independence_clarification_20260926.md). It does not admit any root to evaluation,change the population,inference unit,policy or endpoint,or release collection. It prevents the ambiguous word "untouched" from either overstating separation or making required evaluator audits logically impossible.

## Exposure fields to record separately

For every proposed family,record source inspection,reference/control measurement development,receiver-policy development outcomes,and access to the eventual evaluation outcomes as separate fields,each with exact artifact/version/date provenance. Include related prior roots,not just identical task IDs. A missing record is unknown,not a negative finding.

| Exposure | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Public descriptions,reference source or assertions read by the scientific team | Disclose source inspection. Do not claim source-blind task selection or no exposure in receiver pretraining. It is not an observed receiver policy result. |
| Reference/control audit used to define or check an endpoint | Disclose measurement development and freeze any resulting endpoint version before receiver outcomes. It does not by itself train or evaluate the policy. |
| Receiver continuations,initial answers,grades or policy contrasts used during development | Retain the whole related family in development. New seeds or a new filename do not restore a family holdout. |
| Evaluation outcomes opened before final policy/analysis choices | The affected data cannot independently validate those choices; label it development/exploratory and preserve its history. No rerun or relabeling restores independence from that choice. |

A source-audited,measurement-checked root can still have unobserved future receiver outcomes. Conversely,unobserved receiver outcomes do not make a source-selected root representative,novel,independent or immune to benchmark memorization. The nine-root panel currently has source inspection and measurement preparation; its actual reference/control audit is not yet run,and no new receiver-policy outcomes have been collected. Its roster eligibility remains open. Roots877/345 have completed measurement-development outcomes,and877 also has a definite prior sorting-family relationship; these are different reasons to record,not interchangeable flags.

## Required separation for a prospective claim

Before calling anything independent policy evaluation,freeze the exact development/evaluation family partition,source eligibility rules,public contracts,private measurement version,policies and fitting rule,comparators,continuation and seed laws,missingness treatment,inference and finite resource limits. Keep every branch/repeat of a root and related family on the same side. The scientific team may inspect references to validate the evaluator; that access must not be misrepresented as blinding.

Any task-specific intervention,feature,policy exception,split or threshold chosen using a proposed evaluation task's source constitutes task-source adaptation and must be disclosed. Generic policies fixed earlier are not made source-blind retroactively. Their relation to the evaluation task descriptions and any later changes must remain auditable. A model-training exposure guarantee is unavailable from repository inspection and is not an assumption made by this project.

The current nine-root contract is measurement preparation only. It may not be promoted automatically after references pass. The16 plausible family relations remain held; no inability to resolve them permits silently treating them as independent. The ten no-direct-relation findings remain limited search evidence,and176's contract conflict remains a hold. Any eventual family eligibility ruling needs a documented operational grouping and an explicit residual uncertainty statement; no finite lexical/source audit proves absence of all semantic relations.

## Statistical boundary

If the task roster,measurement rules and selected policy are functions of pre-evaluation information F,and future complete evaluation random inputs have the stipulated product law conditional on F,then the conditional fixed-roster argument already reviewed in LEAD-FRAME-04 applies. Reference/control audits may be part of F; their inclusion does not itself establish or invalidate that product-law assumption. This is an application of the existing conditional argument,not a new theorem. Random common server state,adaptive scheduling or shared failures can still violate the required law. No conditional-independence claim follows from unseen filenames or source-audit timing alone.

That fixed-roster fact still does not replace the original untouched-family/population objective. Operational family separation,sampling from a declared target population,and any transport beyond the sampled frame require their own argument. If the only feasible next result concerns a source-selected finite panel,declare that distinct scope before outcomes and retain the original goal as unmet. Do not widen a limited result in the manuscript or count measurement tests as independent policy evidence.
