# Sampling-law decision for the broad task-family estimand

Codex scientific lead, 30 September 2026. This is a target-identification decision, not a benchmark selection, experiment, or population amendment. Preserve the broad equal-family objective and the strict five-point usefulness rule.

## What equal-family weighting does and does not specify

Equal weighting defines how to aggregate a *specified* set or distribution of semantic task families. It does not define which families exist, their support, or how they enter the study. In the current abstract target, `theta = E_G[D_g]`, the family law `G` is not operationally defined. With no restriction on `G` outside the observed families, two target laws can agree on every saved experiment and assign different mass to unseen families with opposite policy contrasts; they therefore yield different `theta`. This is the ordinary target-support problem, not a shortage of accelerator capacity and not a new theorem.

## Decision and exact next gate

No currently documented source or sampling route identifies the preserved broad-family expectation. Keep the paper's abstract equal-family estimand and report that it is not yet empirically operationalized. Do not create a composite benchmark mixture: its source weights would themselves define a new population. Do not replace family sampling with uniform task-ID sampling, since that generally changes family weights.

To reopen empirical design, a target owner must provide or authorize access to a prospective stream that genuinely represents the intended task-family domain, together with a sampling mechanism and source-independent family crosswalk. The lead can then freeze the stream-relative equal-family estimand explicitly, preserving the five-point threshold, and apply the measurement gate in [`broad_task_family_measurement_gate_20260930.md`](broad_task_family_measurement_gate_20260930.md). If no such stream is available, the scientific next step is a theoretical/design paper with the empirical claim explicitly untested; available GPUs cannot identify an unspecified family law.

No model/task experiment, policy fit, source substitution, or benchmark execution is released by this decision. Readiness remains **60%, delta 0**. Population/measurement, practical policy fit, independent evaluation, and manuscript/raw reproduction remain incomplete.
