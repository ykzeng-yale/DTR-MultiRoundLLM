## 1. The target, exactly as the lead specified it

For fixed realized histories `H = (h_1, …, h_G)`, the **conditional response-mean parameter** is

```
theta_H = (1/G) * sum_g [ E(Y_D | h_g, frozen receiver law) - E(Y_N | h_g, frozen receiver law) ]
```

where `Y_D` and `Y_N` are the frozen-endpoint binary scores under the DIRECTED and NEUTRAL
instruction, `h_g` is root `g`'s realized history (rendered public task prompt, the model's own
generated previous answer, the rendered public diagnostic), and the receiver law is frozen.

Its **all-assigned R-draw sample contrast** is a different object:

```
d_hat = (1/G) * sum_g [ (1/R) * sum_{r=1..R} Y_D(g,r) - (1/R) * sum_{r=1..R} Y_N(g,r) ]
```

with `G = 10`, `R = 6`, 120 continuation slots.

**A sample statistic does not become a causal mean by definition.** `d_hat` is an average of
realized graded outcomes. Reading it as an estimate of `theta_H` requires, and does not establish:

1. **Consistency / state restoration.** Each draw's realized prompt bytes, receiver version,
   sampler settings and container state must be the ones the target conditions on, and the
   assistant-turn history must be restored identically across arms at a root. Byte-identical prefix
   construction is checkable; equality of unobserved receiver/host state is assumed.
2. **No interference.** One slot's outcome must not depend on which other slots were drawn or in
   what order. Shared-host scheduling, cache state and sequence effects are not measured, so this is
   an assumption, not a verified property.
3. **The declared receiver-sampling assumptions.** Replicates at a root are *assumed* independent
   given the frozen prefix and the derived label seed. The only mechanical check available is a
   preflight refusing any collision between newly derived seed values and any label ever spent at
   that root. **Seeds are reproducibility settings, not proof of independent draws.**

Three further separations, stated so no later reading can inflate the target:

- **A fixed task roster can still have a well-defined mean over fresh-prefix randomness.** Defining
  `theta_H`, or a mean over the initial-generation draw at a fixed roster, does **not** require
  random task sampling. The absence of a task-sampling law is not an objection to the parameter's
  existence; it is a limit on what the parameter is about.
- **Broader task-population generalization needs a population/sampling or transport argument.**
  The roster is the deterministic survivor of prior-seen removal, near-duplicate screening,
  provisional family collapse, mechanical criteria and a source-only semantic review, ordered by
  `sha256("mrl15-frame-seed-20260922:<task_id>")`. Hash rank is a preregistration device, not a
  sampling law. No unbiasedness for any task-population mean and no design-based variance is claimed
  at any G.
- **Independent policy value needs a separate frozen policy design.** This stage compares two
  instruction strings at development histories. It evaluates no policy, and its contrast may not be
  transferred to a full-policy comparator or to the later 0.05 useful-gain threshold, which was
  defined for a different contrast and is **not armed here**.

**Design as specified.** Ten roots; per root one fresh initial call, then a public-only diagnostic,
then two arms × six draws. Arm NEUTRAL is `diagnostic.N_INSTRUCTION`; arm DIRECTED is
`diagnostic.select_s1(diag)`, a deterministic function of **public** case statuses only (verified:
`select_s1` reads `diag["cases"][*]["status"]` and returns `S1_STRINGS[0]`, `[1]` or `[2]`). Both
arms share a byte-identical prefix — public task prompt, the model's own previous answer **retained**,
one shared public-diagnostic message object — and both receive the **same** terminal instruction v2
appended. Only the arm instruction differs; a run whose arms differ in any other byte is invalid.
**10 initial + 120 continuation = 130 call slots, all fixed before collection.**

## 5. Inference boundaries (binding)

**Released, descriptively only:** the finite sample contrast `d_hat`; per-root counts (per root,
per arm, successes out of assigned draws); and all-assigned **completion bounds** obtained by
substituting 0 and 1 for each missing slot. Nothing else.

**Not released, and why:**

- **No normal confidence interval.** Not calibrated at `G = 10` selected fixed histories with
  unresolved family structure.
- **No Hoeffding interval.** It requires independence, or a dependence-specific result that has not
  been supplied; **the proposed radius is not assumption-free**. At these G values a `[-1,1]`-bounded
  distribution-free radius is in any case vacuous.
- **No efficacy claim and no futility claim** at this development stage, in either direction. A
  descriptive stage cannot declare a gain established and cannot declare one impossible.
- **Hypothetical normal scenarios are planning material only.** The v2 sizing table (built from
  `scripts/e13_sizing.py:precision` on hypothetical `tau2` values, with `WITHIN_VAR = 0.25`, which
  that script documents as the Bernoulli maximum for one replicate) is retained as planning
  arithmetic. It neither proves adequate precision nor
  proves precision is impossible, and its half-width and radius columns are **not** intervals for
  this stage. v2's arithmetic point stands: the within-root term `2 * 0.25 / R` is `0.083333` at
  `R = 6`, so replicates **do** reduce the per-root standard deviation; the v1 claim that only extra
  roots help remains retracted.
- **Seeds are reproducibility settings**, not proof of independent draws (restated from section 1).
- **Every branch slot is included with probability 1** under the complete-branch design. That is not
  a binary treatment propensity, and **no inverse-propensity weighting** is applicable, needed or
  permitted. Root inclusion is likewise deterministic given the frozen source rule, which is exactly
  why no design-based population inference follows.
- **Caps fixed without inspecting scores prohibit score-driven stopping, but do NOT make missingness
  score-independent.** Runtime and failure modes may correlate with potential scores — a slow or
  crash-prone slot is plausibly a harder slot. Hence: keep every assignment; **suppress the primary
  point contrast if ANY primary branch grade is missing**; report binary completion bounds with **no
  missing-at-random assumption**; and never present those bounds as confidence intervals.
- **No parseability-selected success rate** and no conditioning on any post-treatment output
  property. The output-state partition is reported as a mutually exclusive, exhaustive description of
  where zeros occur, identifying no mediation.
- **Observed saturation is not a floor or a ceiling.** A `0/6` or `6/6` cell is an observation at six
  draws.

**Missingness rules, binding and predeclared** (score-independent, restating the lead's ruling):
keep every assignment with a status and a reason; a **failed initial generation leaves its twelve
continuation slots missing** — do not fabricate an answer, substitute a task-only intervention, retry
or remove the root; a **valid-but-incomplete public diagnostic** uses the predeclared public-only
incomplete-information instruction (`S1_STRINGS[1]` in the DIRECTED arm, unchanged NEUTRAL text in
the other); environment/containment and receiver-law failures keep their existing stop rules;
**candidate private scoring starts only after collection is closed**; **no outcome-based roster
change and no tuning mid-batch**. Balanced order is scheduling only and is inferentially inert.
