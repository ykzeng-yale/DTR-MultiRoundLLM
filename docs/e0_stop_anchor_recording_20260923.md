Structural queries remain separate from observed training support. Exclusive temporary-file publication, flushed journal records and a ceiling of at most 256 MiB protect retained output. This is an output bound, not a wall-time or memory supervisor.

Writer exit and supervised-run completion are explicitly unverified. A complete journal is not evidence that an independently supervised study ran.
