# LEAD-POLICY-02: the fixed comparator is not yet supported as claimed

Coordinating scientific lead, 24 September 2026. **Read-only source and reused-development-result audit; no new trial or release.** The original target remains an independently evaluated public-history rule for the *next prompt at the same prefix*. This audit checks whether E12's saved arms already supply the “competent fixed continuation” required by the [policy design](independent_prompt_policy_validation_design_20260922.md). Competence is not established. **Correction:** [LEAD-POLICY-05](policy_action_unit_correction_20260924.md) shows that E12 does support a development comparison of fixed **recipe indices** N1 and S1 at matched prefixes; S1 is not a fixed literal instruction string. The negative E11/E12/E13a findings remain valid in their stated scope.

The pinned [renderer](../experiments/landmark/diagnostic.py), SHA256 `2a98bccf20cf3fa068790b488be8df4347387ae5ffb1bd7b1bd8b9d49d3a72b2`, gives N1 and S1 the same initial answer and public-diagnostic message before their final instruction. N1 then uses one status-invariant neutral instruction; S1 is one fixed **recipe index** whose rule chooses one of three strings based on the public cases. The saved [E12 diagnostics](../results/e12_dev_v3_20260922T030255Z/B/diagnostics.json), SHA256 `e95d50bae632f73a96094673a03aff9401993c5c3d986086f63c5fe3cf8bd94a`, invoke the S1 public-pass string on nine roots, the recorded-failure string on five, and the incomplete-information string on zero. Therefore E12 supports N1 versus S1 as whole matched-prefix recipe indices, but not outcomes for each S1 constituent string as a **literal fixed instruction across all statuses**. R1 also has a status-invariant diagnostic-enabled instruction, but drops the prior answer and therefore leaves the same-prefix comparison. N1 is the only status-invariant literal instruction within the E12 same-prefix, diagnostic-enabled arms; this does not make it a certified competent baseline.

N0 is status-invariant but omits the diagnostic message from the receiver prefix; it is a different context intervention, not an information-matched next-instruction comparator for N1/S1. In the pinned [E12 analysis](../results/e12_dev_v3_20260922T030255Z/analysis_report.json), SHA256 `ca250a3ea3d5bfe275a202f10b58d3c26a247fba5c63798ee56aa53760eaaf8b`, N0 and N1 each score 20/28 and have per-root means equal to STOP on all fourteen development roots. The saved **hypothetical per-policy** accounting gives N0 two model calls, 448.29 prompt tokens, 85.21 completion tokens and zero public-diagnostic starts per root; N1 gives two calls, 626.86 prompt tokens, 93.43 completion tokens and one public-diagnostic start. These are descriptive realized averages, not population cost estimates or a claim of stochastic dominance. The actual study ran a shared diagnostic for every root; its research expenditure must remain separate from these deployment scenarios. N0 may be a useful *practical* baseline with its own context/cost contract, but inserting it as `b1` silently changes the same-prefix instruction-choice comparison.

**Corrected decision.** N1 is a matched-exposure **mechanism control**, and E12 supports development selection of N1 over S1 within the two **fixed recipe indices**. That narrow selection does not establish a competent fixed policy, a learned selector or independent benefit. Do not use E12 or the proposed N1-versus-S1 E14 panel to claim a learned selector beats a competent development-selected baseline. Before a policy trial, declare the supported recipe-index set and its public rendering, predeclare family-weighted selection and tie breaking (including total resource cost) on **development** families, then lock one `b1` before untouched-family evaluation. The learned `d` must choose from the same supported recipes at the same prefix. A literal-string factorial across public statuses is needed only for the distinct scientific question of constant text effects; do not impose it as a hidden requirement of the original fixed-recipe target. Keep N0 and the bare-task independent-sampling controller as separately labeled practical comparators, with complete cost and stopping laws. No evaluation root, private grade or partial confirmatory outcome may be used to repair these definitions after the freeze.

This removes a specific false shortcut but does **not** complete the trial freeze: the untouched family frame, source-valid endpoint, selector training, assignment/seed laws, useful-gain/precision/futility analysis and numerical local cap remain unresolved. E14, source renewal and all model collection remain **HOLD**. This audit read committed text/JSON and ran only scalar/count checks: zero new receiver calls or tokens, candidate/reference/benchmark executions, fits, synthetic draws or paid spend. **58%, change 0 percentage points** under the fixed rubric; prompt efficacy is unestablished and the full project is not submission-ready.
